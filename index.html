Common Questions

What happens after I decide to start a Screenbridge project?

Once you’re ready to move forward, you’ll complete a secure payment for your selected package — Standard, Premium, or Signature — plus any add-ons you choose. After checkout, you’ll get a short Launch Form to share your creative assets and brand details. Your project manager will reach out to introduce themselves and kick off the creative process.

What kind of content should I send to begin my project?

We can work from a finished social ad or raw creator footage, though raw footage is highly preferred for the best results. Send us clean clips without captions, stickers, or music, along with your logo, brand guidelines, and any key messages or calls to action you’d like featured.

How long does it take to deliver my Screenbridge ad?

Timelines start once we’ve received all assets and approvals:
Standard: 10 business days
Premium: 15 business days
Signature: 20 business days
Need it sooner? Rush options are available.

Can I use my Screenbridge ad on multiple platforms?

Absolutely. Your ad can run across TV and Connected TV (CTV), YouTube, Amazon, Product Detail Pages (PDPs), Online Video (OLV), and all major paid social platforms. We also offer social-specific exports in square or vertical formats as optional add-ons.

Do you offer different ad lengths or cutdowns?

Yes. We can create ads in whatever length best fits your channel strategy.
For TV and CTV, the standard is 15 or 30 seconds. YouTube often performs well with longer formats, while social platforms favor shorter, faster cuts.
Custom lengths and alternate edits are available as add-ons.

What if I don’t have existing video content?

We offer full production support for both video and photo shoots — including scripting, directing, and shot listing. Depending on your needs, we can assist your production team or handle the shoot ourselves. Set up a quick call with us to discuss what works best for your project.

How involved will I be in the creative process?

You’ll be involved at every key stage. Your team will review and give feedback at each milestone before we move forward. This ensures alignment throughout the process — so there are no surprises and your final ad reflects exactly what you envisioned.

What rights do I have to my final Screenbridge ad?

Once full payment is made, you receive a worldwide, perpetual license to use the final ad on all agreed platforms.
We retain ownership of the project files and templates used in production so we can offer consistent updates or future versions if needed.

Do you offer subscriptions or bundle packages for ongoing campaigns?

Yes. We offer both multi-month campaign plans and bundle packages for brands running recurring or multi-ad campaigns. These plans include flexible scheduling and built-in discounts. Contact us to learn more about what fits your campaign needs.

Why choose Screenbridge for your ads?

Screenbridge transforms creator and UGC footage into high-performing, broadcast-quality ads for TV, CTV, and digital platforms.
You’ll get cinematic results, fast turnaround, and transparent pricing — without agency overhead.
