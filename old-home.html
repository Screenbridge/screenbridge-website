Common Questions

What is Connected TV (CTV), and how is it different from traditional TV?

CTV refers to television content streamed over the internet, bypassing traditional cable or satellite providers. It allows brands to target specific audiences more effectively compared to traditional TV ads.

Why should I turn my social media ads into CTV ads?

Repurposing your social media ads for CTV extends their reach to a broader, highly engaged audience. It’s a cost-effective way to amplify your message and connect with viewers on their favorite streaming platforms.

What kind of social media ads work best for CTV?

Most high-performing video ads, including Instagram Reels, TikToks, or Facebook videos, can be adapted for CTV. We help ensure they’re optimized for the big screen.

Will the CTV ads still look like my social media ads?

Yes! We preserve the authentic style and feel of your social media ads while enhancing them to suit the TV format, ensuring they look professional and impactful.

How long does the process take?

Typically, we can transform your social media ads into CTV-ready formats within a few days, depending on the complexity and volume of the content.

Which streaming platforms will my ads appear on?

Your ads can run on popular streaming platforms like Hulu, Roku, Amazon Fire TV, and others, depending on your campaign setup.

Can I target specific audiences with my CTV ads?

Absolutely. CTV advertising offers precise targeting options, allowing you to reach specific demographics, locations, and viewer behaviors.

Do I need to create new content for CTV?

Not necessarily. Screenbridge specializes in repurposing your existing social media ads, saving you time and production costs.

What file types or formats do you need for the ads?

We accept most standard video formats like MP4, MOV, or files directly exported from platforms like Instagram or TikTok.

Can you help with running the ads on CTV platforms?

Yes! We provide support to ensure your ads are ready to stream and can guide you through the distribution process.
