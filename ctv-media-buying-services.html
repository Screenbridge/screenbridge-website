Common Questions

What is Screenbridge+?

Screenbridge+ is Screenbridge’s integrated creative + media management program. It combines premium, made-to-order CTV creative with full media buying, optimization, and performance strategy, all under one system. Brands get production, testing, and iteration without the cost or friction of traditional creative productions separated from the media agency.

How does pricing for Screenbridge+ work?

Screenbridge+ uses a simple, performance-aligned model:

When you run your media through Screenbridge or one of our partners, your creative is included. You commit to a monthly media budget, and we take a small management fee.

This eliminates upfront production costs and ensures creative and media work in total alignment.

What are the deliverables included with “Creative Included”?

Each creative package includes everything you need to launch on CTV quickly and professionally:
‍
    • A 30-second CTV commercial
    • A 15-second cut-down
    • Optional short-form variations
    • Optional alternate hook versions
    • Optional alternate end cards / CTAs
    • Platform-ready exports for Roku, Hulu, Amazon, YouTube, OLV, and more

Additional edits and variations can be added depending on your goals or tier.

What type of creative does Screenbridge produce?

Screenbridge produces custom, made-to-order CTV ads created by our in-house team of editors, animators, producers, and writers with thousands of real ad experience.

No cheap AI Templates. No shortcuts.Every ad is crafted to be premium, on-brand, and designed for maximum performance.

What are Screenbridge’s creative tiers?

Screenbridge offers three tiers of creative, each designed for different goals:

Standard: Ideal for turning a single piece of strong UGC into a clean, performance-focused CTV spot.
‍
Premium: A more elevated, storytelling-driven version of your UGC, using best practices for CTV structure, pacing, and brand polish.
‍
Signature: Our highest-end tier, designed for campaigns that need multiple personas, multiple products, narrative layers, and enhanced branding elements.

Depending on your media spend tier, one of these is included in your Screenbridge+ program.

How fast does Screenbridge deliver a finished CTV ad?

Most brands receive their full creative package in a few days to a few weeks, depending on the complexity of the ad and the speed at which assets are provided.This is significantly faster than traditional video production, which often takes 6–12 weeks.

Does Screenbridge use AI to make the ads?

No, Screenbridge is not an AI video generator.
Your ads are crafted by the same award-winning creative team behind Filmkraft.tv, using real editors, animators, producers, and copywriters.

We do use selective AI tools behind the scenes to speed up workflows and generate creative elements when the quality is maintained (like AI Voiceovers).

Why use Screenbridge instead of an AI video tool?

AI video tools produce quick, templated, or synthetic content that often feels generic or off-brand, fine for simple social posts, but not suitable for premium TV placements or serious performance campaigns.

Screenbridge is for brands that need:
‍
    • Premium, on-brand, human-made creative
    • CTV-ready formatting
    • Better storytelling and structure
    • Higher credibility and trust on the big screen
    • A real team optimizing and iterating over time

If you care about brand perception, polish, and performance on television, Screenbridge is the right fit.‍

Who should use Screenbridge?

Screenbridge is ideal for:

    • Growth-stage DTC brands
    • Premium consumer brands
    • Companies with strong UGC or influencer content
    • Brands investing in CTV, YouTube, or omni-channel video
    • Teams who need production + media under one aligned system

Screenbridge sits in the middle between AI tools (fast but low quality) and large production agencies (beautiful but expensive/slow).

We deliver premium performance creative at speed, without the cost of traditional productions.‍

What platforms can Screenbridge ads run on?

All Screenbridge creative is delivered in platform-ready formats for:

    • CTV (Roku, Hulu, Amazon, Samsung, etc.)
    • YouTube and Online Video
    • Retail media PDPs
    • Meta/TikTok cutdowns (if requested)
    • Linear TV compatibility

You can deploy the same creative across multiple channels for unified performance.

What platforms can Screenbridge ads run on?

Screenbridge+ includes:

    • Media planning and targeting
    • Campaign setup and launch
    • Ongoing optimization and tuning
    • Weekly or bi-weekly reporting (depending on tier)

    • Persona expansion and hook testing
    • Full funnel performance reviews

Creative + media are tightly integrated so the entire flywheel runs faster.

Can Screenbridge help with social ads too?

Yes.
While CTV is our core product, many clients repurpose their Screenbridge creative for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and more. We can provide short-form variations or cutdowns depending on your needs.

Is there a minimum spend to use Screenbridge+?

Yes. minimums vary by tier.
Screenbridge supports brands with smaller daily budgets up to enterprises spending six figures monthly. Your tier determines which creative package you receive and how frequently you get optimization.

We’ll recommend the right tier during your intro call.

How does the Screenbridge creative process work?

Screenbridge uses a performance-first workflow:
‍
     1. We review your existing UGC and top-performing ads
     2. We rebuild them into premium horizontal CTV formats
     3. We launch quickly to gather data
     4. We iterate with new hooks, angles, personas, or CTAs
     5. We scale what works across channels

This produces fast learnings, strong performance, and long-term creative improvement.
