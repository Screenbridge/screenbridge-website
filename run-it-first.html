Common Questions

How can you offer the creative for free?

We're confident in the work and we'd rather prove it first. If you run the ad, we share in the upside through a simple monthly usage fee.

What counts as "a month of usage"?

If the ad runs at least once during a month (or 30-day period), that period is billable. If you pause it, billing pauses.

How do you track whether the ad is running?

We use standard reporting: platform logs, partner reporting, or screenshots/exported reports—whatever is easiest. We'll align on the method during onboarding.

Who owns the creative?

You receive usage rights to the final deliverables (worldwide, perpetual, for agreed channels). Screenbridge retains ownership of internal templates/project files used to produce the work—so we can efficiently version and update your ads.

What kind of content should I send to begin my project?

We can work from a finished social ad or raw creator footage, though raw footage is highly preferred for the best results. Send us clean clips without captions, stickers, or music, along with your logo, brand guidelines, and any key messages or calls to action you'd like featured.

How long does it take to deliver my Screenbridge ad?

Timelines start once we've received all assets and approvals:
Standard: 10 business days
Premium: 15 business days
Signature: 20 business days
Need it sooner? Rush options are available.

Can I use my Screenbridge ad on multiple platforms?

Absolutely. Your ad can run across TV and Connected TV (CTV), YouTube, Amazon, Product Detail Pages (PDPs), Online Video (OLV), and all major paid social platforms. We also offer social-specific exports in square or vertical formats as optional add-ons.

How involved will I be in the creative process?

You'll be involved at every key stage. Your team will review and give feedback at each milestone before we move forward. This ensures alignment throughout the process — so there are no surprises and your final ad reflects exactly what you envisioned.

What if I don't have existing video content?

We offer full production support for both video and photo shoots — including scripting, directing, and shot listing. Depending on your needs, we can assist your production team or handle the shoot ourselves. Set up a quick call with us to discuss what works best for your project.
